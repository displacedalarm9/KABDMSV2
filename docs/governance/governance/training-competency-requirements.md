# UNISYS Training & Competency Requirements

## Purpose

This document defines the training and competency requirements for personnel and agents operating within the UNISYS system. It ensures that all users have the knowledge and skills required to perform their responsibilities correctly and consistently.

---

## 1. Scope

These requirements apply to:

- All human operators and administrators
- All agents executing automated UNISYS processes
- External contributors and reviewers
- Anyone with access to governed UNISYS artifacts

---

## 2. Competency Domains

### 2.1 Core UNISYS Competencies (All Personnel)

| Competency | Description | Assessment Method |
|------------|-------------|-------------------|
| UNISYS Identity Framework | Understanding of UNISYS IDs, naming, and registration | Written assessment |
| Document Control Basics | How to create, find, and use controlled documents | Practical exercise |
| Lifecycle Model | Understanding artifact lifecycle stages | Written assessment |
| Change Control Awareness | When and how to initiate a change request | Written assessment |

### 2.2 Governance Role Competencies

| Competency | Applicable Role | Assessment Method |
|------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Change Control Process | CCB Members | Practical exercise |
| Deviation Management | QA/Governance staff | Scenario-based assessment |
| CAPA Process | QA/Governance staff | Case study |
| Audit Procedures | Auditors | Practical audit simulation |

### 2.3 Technical Role Competencies

| Competency | Applicable Role | Assessment Method |
|------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Node Configuration | System Administrators | Hands-on demonstration |
| Validation Protocols | Validation Engineers | Protocol execution review |
| PMF Maintenance | All technical staff | Practical exercise |
| Traceability Matrix Maintenance | Requirements/Test Engineers | Practical exercise |

---

## 3. Training Requirements by Role

### 3.1 System Administrator

Required training:

1. UNISYS Identity Constitution
2. Node Configuration Standards
3. Change Control Process
4. Operational Architecture (Boot Sequence, Runtime Model)
5. Error Handling & Recovery
6. Logging & Monitoring Standard

### 3.2 Governance Staff

Required training:

1. UNISYS Identity Constitution
2. Document Control & Records Management
3. Change Control Process
4. Deviation Management
5. CAPA Process
6. Audit & Inspection Readiness
7. Traceability Matrix Standard

### 3.3 Validation Engineer

Required training:

1. UNISYS Identity Constitution
2. Requirements Specification Standard
3. IQ/OQ/PQ protocols
4. Test Plan & Protocol Standard
5. Validation Report Template
6. Traceability Matrix Standard

### 3.4 General User / Contributor

Required training:

1. UNISYS Identity Constitution (overview)
2. Document Control Basics
3. Lifecycle Model (overview)

---

## 4. Training Records

Training completion shall be documented with:

- Trainee name/ID
- Training topic and document reference
- Training date
- Training method (self-study, instructor-led, practical)
- Assessment result (Pass/Fail or score)
- Trainer/assessor name

Training records are retained per the Document Control & Records Management standard.

---

## 5. Competency Assessment

### 5.1 Initial Qualification

Before performing governed activities, personnel must:

1. Complete required training for their role
2. Pass the applicable competency assessment
3. Have training record filed

### 5.2 Periodic Requalification

| Trigger | Retraining Required |
|---------|---------------------|
| Annual review cycle | Core competencies review |
| Process or standard update | Updated process training |
| Deviation involving human error | Targeted retraining |
| Role change | New role competency training |

### 5.3 Competency Gaps

Identified competency gaps shall be:

1. Documented
2. Addressed with targeted training
3. Reassessed before returning to governed activities

---

## 6. Training for Automated Agents

Automated agents (e.g., AI systems, bots) shall:

- Operate only within their defined scope
- Have capabilities documented and verified before operational use
- Be re-verified after system updates
- Have operational logs that are auditable
