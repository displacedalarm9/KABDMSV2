# UNISYS Audit & Inspection Readiness

## Purpose

This document defines the standards and practices for maintaining audit and inspection readiness within the UNISYS system. It ensures that the system can demonstrate compliance at any time and that audit activities produce meaningful results.

---

## 1. Scope

This document covers:

- Internal governance audits
- Validation and qualification inspections
- External reviews or assessments
- Self-assessments and readiness checks

---

## 2. Readiness Principles

1. **Always-Ready Posture** — The UNISYS system maintains documentation and records such that an audit could be conducted at any time with minimal preparation
2. **Evidence-Based Compliance** — All compliance claims are supported by documented evidence
3. **Traceability** — Every governance artifact is traceable to its requirements and validation evidence
4. **Accessibility** — Audit evidence is organized, accessible, and retrievable within defined timelines

---

## 3. Audit Readiness Checklist

### 3.1 Document Control

- [ ] All current documents are approved and versioned
- [ ] Superseded documents are clearly marked and archived
- [ ] Document index is current and complete
- [ ] PMFs are maintained for all governed artifacts

### 3.2 Change Control

- [ ] All change requests are documented
- [ ] Change records include implementation and verification evidence
- [ ] No unauthorized changes (changes without CR)
- [ ] Emergency changes are documented and post-reviewed

### 3.3 Deviation & CAPA

- [ ] All deviations are documented in Deviation Records
- [ ] All CAPAs are tracked to closure
- [ ] CAPA effectiveness verifications are documented
- [ ] CAPA trend analysis is current

### 3.4 Validation

- [ ] IQ/OQ/PQ records are complete and approved
- [ ] Traceability matrices are current
- [ ] Validation reports are filed and accessible
- [ ] Re-validation events are tracked and scheduled

### 3.5 Training

- [ ] Training records are current for all personnel
- [ ] Competency assessments are documented
- [ ] Training curriculum is up to date with current standards

### 3.6 Operational Records

- [ ] System logs are retained and accessible
- [ ] Incident records are documented
- [ ] Node configuration records are current

---

## 4. Audit Types

### 4.1 Internal Audit

Conducted by UNISYS governance staff to verify compliance with internal standards.

**Frequency:** Quarterly

**Scope:** Rotating coverage of all governance layers; full system audit annually.

**Output:** Audit report with findings, observations, and recommendations.

### 4.2 Self-Assessment

Conducted by artifact owners or process owners.

**Frequency:** As triggered by process changes or readiness concerns.

**Output:** Self-assessment checklist with identified gaps and action items.

### 4.3 External Review

Conducted by parties outside the UNISYS governance structure.

**Preparation requirements:**
- Pre-audit document package prepared
- Subject matter experts identified and available
- Evidence files organized by audit scope
- Escort and logistics arranged

---

## 5. Audit Finding Classification

| Level | Description | Response Time |
|-------|-------------|---------------|
| Critical | Non-compliance with safety or fundamental governance requirement | Immediate CAPA; activity halt if necessary |
| Major | Significant gap in compliance or process | CAPA within 15 business days |
| Minor | Limited or isolated non-compliance | Corrective action within 30 business days |
| Observation | Opportunity for improvement, not a compliance gap | Consider for next planning cycle |

---

## 6. Audit Records

Audit records must include:

- Audit number and date
- Audit type and scope
- Auditor(s)
- Checklist completed
- Findings (with classifications)
- Responses and commitments
- Follow-up verification record
- Closure date

Audit records are retained per the Document Control & Records Management standard.

---

## 7. Pre-Audit Preparation

When an audit is scheduled:

1. Identify audit scope and applicable governance layer(s)
2. Compile evidence package for the scope
3. Complete pre-audit self-assessment checklist
4. Address any identified gaps before audit date (if time permits)
5. Brief relevant personnel
